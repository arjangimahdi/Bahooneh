# 01 — Product Vision & Business Model

## Vision Statement

> *"Never stare at a blank search bar wondering what to buy someone again. Giftway turns 'I have no idea what to get them' into a curated, purchasable shortlist in under 15 seconds."*

## Core Problem

Gift-giving is emotionally high-stakes but informationally low-signal. Users know a lot about the recipient (implicitly) but struggle to translate that knowledge into a product search query. This creates **"gift anxiety"** — a mix of decision paralysis, fear of giving a bad/generic gift, and time pressure (often last-minute).

Generic e-commerce search fails here because:

- Search bars require the user to already know *what* they want, not *who* they're buying for.
- Existing "gift guide" content (blog listicles) is static, not personalized, and not tied to real-time inventory or pricing.
- Marketplaces optimize for products the user has already decided on — not for the ambiguous, emotional, constraint-heavy decision of gifting.

## Business Model — Affiliate Intermediary

Giftway does **not** hold inventory, process payments, or fulfill orders. It is a pure **recommendation and referral layer**:

| Aspect | Model |
|---|---|
| Revenue mechanism | Affiliate commission on completed purchases via outbound tracked links |
| Partner type | Local e-commerce giants (e.g., Digikala, Snapshop, and future partners) |
| User payment flow | None — user is redirected to the partner site/app to complete checkout |
| Core value exchange | Partners get high-intent, pre-qualified traffic; Giftway gets commission; user gets a curated shortlist instead of an open-ended search |

### Why this matters for product decisions

Because Giftway earns only on click-through → conversion, every product decision should be evaluated against two questions:

1. Does this increase the *quality* of the match (so the user actually clicks and buys)?
2. Does this reduce *friction* between "AI recommends" and "user clicks affiliate link"?

This reframes the entire MVP as an **intent-refinement funnel**, not a content or catalog product.
